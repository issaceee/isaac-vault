
Writeup on how we were taught to study literature:
- TEM - Technique, effect, meaning - for every piece of analysis
- Very structured - formula-esque ways of structuring our essays and our points (Topic Sentences must have How + Why)
- We didn't really bring in historical context as part of close reading, it was more to substantiate the further meaning and wider themes of the text rather than to act as a substantiate to the technique or effects use
	- some focus on local literature and its colloquilisms
