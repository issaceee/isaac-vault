Old English Poetry
- every line is made up by 2 half lines, seperated by a caesura
	- **e**ce drihten,        **æ**fter teode.  (regular alliteration)
	- **f**irum **f**oldan,         **f**rea ælmihtig.  (double alliteration - shows a poet going through extra kind of effort to exert this effect/emphasize a particular point, usually when talking abt art,music,life,beauty etc. mimesis btw the ornamentation of the verse and the ornamentation of the narrative)
	- on-verse.       caesura.     off-verse
- alliterated words have significance, signifies connection
