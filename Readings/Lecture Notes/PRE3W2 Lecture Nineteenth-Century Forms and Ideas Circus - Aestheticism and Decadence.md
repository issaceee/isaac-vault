## Walter Pater, Father of Aestheticism
- The Renaissance - Stories in Art and Poetry 
	- defines a new way about thinking about art
	- meta level - what does it say abt art, abt the r/s btw life and art?
	- trying to separate art from ethical and moral constraints
		- art on the level of form and perception

		- aesthetic (things that can be sensed by the senses) criticsm
			- **embodied** understanding of art
				- "impression", 
					- the idea of uncertainty is what the critics celebrate, the ambiguity and the unstable/fleeting
					- that its renewed all the time, need to SIEZE that impression
				- "to me", 
					- on the subject, the person who interacts, becomes the center of the aesthetic criticmsm
				- "pleasure"
			- instead of seeing "the object as in itself it really is" [[@arnoldArnoldCultureAnarchy1993]] - but the meaning is in the r/s betw the work of art and the subject - **shift from subjectivity to objectivity**
- Pater's Mona Lisa (1873)
	- Pater is not giving an objective description, but a representation of his impression 
	- not a realistic style --> BUt to ==individualise criticism (mediated through the particular consciousness of the critic)==
	- unique style of writing (long sentences etc.) - big part of his legacy
- WB Yeats sets Pater into verse (1936)
	- Yeats as a major turn of century poet
	- Modern poetry starts with Yeats reconfiguration of Pater into verse

- Patel's Conclusion to Studies in the History of the Renaissance
	- "To burn always with this hard,gemlike flame"
			- Intensity
	- "The love of art for its own sake"
		- relate with art and literature not to improve but so that we can enjoy
		- meaning in art is not in its content but in its form

	- idea of fleeting nature of things (very much in contrast of the ethical of George Elliot and Gaskell of large systems, long-lasting)
	- **Intensity** - -Aestheitcims is sometimes called the gospel of intensity in a world of fleeting moments
	- "strangeness" the addition of strangeness to beuaty (there is an interest–Baudelaire–in the strange combition of Beauty and weirdness,corruption, ugliness (death etc.))
	-  the business of the critic is not to tell u how to think but to deepen the mystery
